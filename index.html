A bit about me

I'm a final-year computer science student in Pune who likes understanding how systems break, so I can build ones that don't.

Security is where I spend most of my time: SIEM monitoring, detection rules and threat hunting. I'm just as happy writing code, whether that's a full-stack app, a CI/CD scanner or a GenAI chatbot.

Away from the screen I trek forts across Maharashtra, train at the gym, and compete in hackathons, CTFs and the occasional debate.
